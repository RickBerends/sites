## About
Florissant Tuinen is a garden company based in Oosterhout with close to 20
years of experience designing and building gardens. Their own garden designer
trained in tuin- en landschapsarchitectuur. They work for private clients and
businesses, and also take on projects for governments and water boards
(overheden, waterschappen), which points to a larger, more established
operation than most businesses in this batch. They serve Oosterhout, Breda and
Tilburg, among other areas. (Source: florissanttuinen.nl — via WebSearch
synthesis)
