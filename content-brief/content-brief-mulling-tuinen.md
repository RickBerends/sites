## About
Mulling Tuinen is a young company founded by Thijs Mulling, based in Tilburg and
active across the Breda–Den Bosch–Eindhoven region. The company works with quiet
electric tools rather than petrol equipment — both a sustainability choice and a
way to keep the noise down for neighbours — and treats the garden as an
extension of nature, aiming for flora and fauna to thrive alongside people.
(Source: mullingtuinen.nl — Over)

## Services
- Tuinonderhoud (garden maintenance) — one-off renovation through to periodic
  maintenance, private and commercial, tuned to the seasons. (source:
  mullingtuinen.nl/diensten)
- Heg- en vormsnoei (hedge & topiary pruning) — from crisp geometric shapes to
  more natural forms, to the client's preference. (source: mullingtuinen.nl/diensten)
- Onkruidverwijdering (weed removal). (source: mullingtuinen.nl/diensten)
