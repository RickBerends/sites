## About
Van Dijk De Hovenier has been a known name in the trade for 25 years (founded
1 October 1995), and has increasingly focused on garden maintenance over that
time. The company helps with garden design or maintenance advice, tree care,
tree removal, and stump grinding. (Sources: vandijkdehovenier.nl,
vakgroep-hoveniers.nl — via WebSearch synthesis)
