## About
Vitamine Groen is run by Ramon Gadellaa, an ecological garden designer who also
teaches Ecological Garden Design at IPD. Working from permaculture principles —
every plant earning its place multiple times over, for bloom, for food, for
insects, for shelter — Ramon designs and builds gardens that increase
biodiversity. Since 2016 the focus has been on connecting people and nature; he
co-designed two public edible/ecological inspiration gardens in Tilburg to show
residents how to green a tiled garden. (Sources: puurpermacultuur.nl,
vitaminegroen.org/missie, vitaminegroen.eco/about — via WebSearch synthesis)
