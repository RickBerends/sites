## About
De Knoest is a landscaping company based in Tilburg, run by owner Thijs
Beirnaert. Gardens are designed by an in-house garden designer and built by a
team of nine gardeners. Beirnaert has deliberately built a team with many women
working in it — unusual in a sector that's mostly men — and treats that as
something the company is proud of. (Sources: deknoest-tuinen.nl, tilburg.com —
via WebSearch synthesis)

## Services
- Tuinontwerp & aanleg (garden design & construction) — planting, sierbestrating,
  houtbouw (terrace/pergola), tuinverlichting, beregening, grondwerk. (source:
  deknoest-tuinen.nl)
- Boomverzorging (tree care) — in-house tree specialists for care and removal of
  trees of any size. (source: deknoest-tuinen.nl)
- Tuinonderhoud (garden maintenance) — for private and business clients.
  (source: deknoest-tuinen.nl)
