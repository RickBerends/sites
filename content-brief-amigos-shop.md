## About
Amigos Shop is a parcel service point and convenience shop on Pater van den
Elsenplein in the Visserijbuurt neighbourhood of Tilburg. It handles parcel
send-and-collect for several major carriers under one roof, and stocks everyday
items — soft drinks, sweets and crisps. It's a small, local, walk-in service
point rather than a large depot.

## Private signal — NOT for publication (shapes tone/priorities only)

repeated "closed without notice", "hours keep changing", "no answer on the
phone." This is the business's clearest reputation risk. Strategic implication
for the site (to discuss with client, not to state on the page): make **accurate,
trustworthy opening hours** and a **clear "call/check before you come" or live
status** the centrepiece, so the site actively counters that pain rather than
inheriting it. This is read-only signal from reviews; no review text is
reproduced on the site.
