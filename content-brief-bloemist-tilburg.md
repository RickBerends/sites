## About
Bloem'ist is a florist shop on the Heuvelstraat in Tilburg's city centre,
occupying a spot previously known as Boetiek Napoleon. Local press described its
opening under the tagline "Tilburg krijgt weer kleur" ("Tilburg gets colour
again"), reintroducing a dedicated flower shop to the city ring after a
competitor's departure. The shop's own Instagram bio describes it simply as a
"Bloemist" offering "Eindelijk weer verse bloemen & planten in hartje Tilburg!"
("Finally fresh flowers & plants in the heart of Tilburg again") plus
bridal/funeral bouquets. Google reviewers describe it as a small, cosy shop with
personal service and a good bouquet selection; one reviewer flagged pricing and
service speed as a downside — noted here as private signal, not reproduced as a
quote (see Testimonials).
